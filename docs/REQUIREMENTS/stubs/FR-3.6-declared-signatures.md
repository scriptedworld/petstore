# FR-3.6, Declared signatures

| ID | Requirement | |
|---|---|---|
| FR-3.6 | A stub shall optionally accept a signature, given as parameter names or copied from a callable, and shall bind invocation arguments to those names. The two sources shall be mutually exclusive, and a stub without a declared signature shall record no bound arguments. | [A] |
